<h1>Unchecked Alibi</h1>

<p><strong>Unchecked Alibi is a first-person 3D narrative mystery game that explores how dementia can affect memory, communication, recognition, and everyday experiences.</strong></p>

<h2>Our Vision</h2>

<p>People living with dementia are vulnerable to mistreatment from caregivers due to a lack of understanding of how to respond to difficult behaviors. The game aims to encourage greater understanding of and empathy by providing examples of supportive ways to interact with people living with dementia. It is primarily designed for people with a personal connection to dementia who want to better understand how to provide support, while also appealing to players who enjoy narrative-driven mystery games.</p>
